Sifa-dent Dental Centre


Sifa-dent Dental Centre is a registered professional dental clinic, located in Mihang'o Utawala on Kiguathi Road at SDA stage.


We offer full-scale dental services in a state -of-the-art clinic


We listen to the needs of our patients and give them same day solutions. We pride ourselves with over 18 years of clinical experience.
